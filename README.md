Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb): one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — built only from things a person from 1800 could picture.

## The sentence

> Think of a road of light linking every post office and library on earth, so a letter you send is read across the world before the ink dries.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), validating that it is a single sentence of at most 200 characters.
